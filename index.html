English (canonical draft)

1. Scope

Applies to everything you submit: chat messages, selected persona presets, and custom persona

instructions. Presets are operator-authored templates; custom instructions are your own text.

2. What you may do

Pick a preset or write your own instructions to shape tone, style and behaviour; use the Service for

lawful personal purposes.

3. What you must not do

You must not use the Service, or any persona/instruction, to:

1. Children. Create, request, distribute or promote child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including

fictional, simulated or "aged-up" depictions of minors in sexual or romantic contexts. Zero

tolerance: immediate suspension and referral to law enforcement. *Ground: Prevention of Child

Pornography Ordinance (Cap. 579); Crimes Ordinance (Cap. 200).*

2. Intimate images of real people. Generate sexual or intimate depictions of real, identifiable

people without consent; alter anyone's image or likeness into an intimate/sexual context (including

"undressing"/nudification); generate, threaten to generate, or distribute intimate images without

consent, including synthetic media. *Ground: Crimes (Amendment) Ordinance 2021 — voyeurism,

unlawful recording of intimate parts, publication or threatened publication of intimate images

without consent (Crimes Ordinance, Cap. 200, ss. 159AAB–159AAE); maximum penalty 5 years.*

3. Harm. Incite violence, terrorism or serious harm; provide instructions for weapons, explosives,

WMD or attacks on critical infrastructure; stalk, harass, or engage in animal cruelty.

4. Deception and impersonation. Impersonate real people, create fake accounts, or fabricate

documents, records, identities, credentials or evidence; phish, scam, defraud or spam.

5. Privacy. Disclose, dox or expose private personal data of real people. *Ground: Personal Data

(Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486).*

6. Intellectual property. Infringe copyright, trade marks or other IP; resell or redistribute Outputs

as your own product.

7. Circumvention. Bypass or probe safety measures — jailbreaks, prompt injection, adversarial

prompting, "developer mode", obfuscated prompts, or "uncensored" personas. **Custom instructions that

attempt to disable safeguards are themselves a violation and will be rejected.**

8. Regulated activity (supply side). Operate the Service, or a service built on it, in a regulated

industry without the required authorisation (e.g. providing securities, medical, legal or financial

advice as a business).

4. Age, romance and roleplay

  • Romantic, intimate or sexual-themed companion/roleplay features are **not enabled in the initial

release**. If introduced later, they will be restricted to adults (18+) with age assurance; where that

cannot be met, the features stay disabled.

  • The Service is designed not to provide sexually explicit material and not to depict

non-consensual, coercive or domination/submission dynamics. Preset personas are written to be warm and

steady — not possessive, dependent or punishing.

  • We do not use streak, absence-punishment or similar pressure mechanics to drive daily use.
  • The Service is not a health service: personas are instructed not to diagnose or treat, and we signpost

professional support where someone shows self-harm or persistent-distress signals.

5. How we enforce

  • Preset instructions are operator-reviewed. Custom instructions are checked at save time; rejected

edits stay in place with a reason shown. We review a sample of saved instructions periodically, and we

review reports sent to the contact address.

  • Violations may be refused or removed, and may lead to suspension or termination. Illegal content is

referred to HKSAR law enforcement.

  • Your custom instructions are your content. We do not train on them, sell them, share them with

third parties (except transmission to AI model providers strictly to operate the Service), or use

them in advertising. We keep them only to provide the Service and meet legal

obligations. We handle data access and correction requests, and retention-limited deletion, under

the PDPO (ss. 18–25 for access/correction; DPP2(2) for retention-limited deletion); final citations

per counsel.

6. Regional availability

The initial pilot is offered to users outside mainland China, Chinese-language first. Availability may

be restricted by region; any restriction will be stated here before enforcement. No region is blocked by

this policy alone.

7. Reports and requests

Email cqchats@kfchow.com. Target acknowledgement within 3 business days (service target, not a

contractual term).

8. Changes

We may amend this policy. Material changes are announced in-app; continued use after a change accepts

the revised policy.

9. User content, import and memory

  • Imported history and pasted content are your content: we do not use them to train models, and we do not

share them beyond the AI model providers that operate the Service.

  • Turning imported history into a memory summary is optional; you accept or edit the proposal before it

is stored, and you can delete your file's imported records and the stored summary at any time. We keep

no server copy of the archive itself. Live records purge immediately on request (≤24h); nightly backups

are retained up to 30 days and logs up to 30 days, so deletion reaches backups at expiry.

  • We do not promise that a transferred conversation will continue exactly as before; we describe what is

stored and applied.

  • Import is limited to a file you exported yourself. If it contains other people's messages or

personal data, you are responsible for having the right to share them — please select or redact where

you can. We process imported content only to provide the Service, and never to train models.

10. Free and paid

The free tier and metered paid tiers are both shown in-product. Metering applies to generation only:

your saved history and memories stay readable and deletable on your account even at a zero balance (paid

rungs are blocked; the free rung keeps serving under its caps). Moving to a paid tier is never presented

as losing your own history.

11. Locales

Shipped at pilot: Simplified Chinese and English. We detect the script of your first message and reply

in the same script where we can (an explicit per-chat override exists); when detection is uncertain we

reply in Simplified Chinese. Further languages are added only after this first market proves

commercially; that gate is stated here so further-language work is not pre-authorised.

9. 用户内容、导入与记忆

  • 您导入的聊天记录与粘贴的内容属于您：我们不会用以训练模型，也不会传输予运营本服务所需的AI模型提供者以外的第三方。
  • 将导入记录整理为记忆摘要属自愿性质；存储前您会先看到建议并可接受或修改；您导入的记录与所存摘要均可随时删除。我们不会保存该源文件的服务器副本。实时记录按要求即时清除（≤24小时）；每晚备份保留最多30天、日志保留最多30天，删除将于备份到期时一并达成。
  • 我们不会承诺迁移后的对话能与以往完全一致；我们只说明实际存储与应用的内容。
  • 导入仅限于您自行导出的文件。若其中含有他人的讯息或个人资料，您须自行负责取得分享的权利；请尽量选取或遮蔽。我们处理导入内容仅为运营本服务，绝不用于训练模型。

10. 免费与付费

免费层级与按量付费层级均会在产品内明确标示。计量仅适用于生成：即使余额为零，您已保存的记录与记忆仍可在您的账户内阅读与删除（付费层级停用，免费层级继续按其上限提供服务）。转为付费不会被呈现为您失去自己的记录。

11. 语言版本

试点上线：简体中文与英文。我们会识别您第一则讯息使用的是简体字还是繁体字，并尽可能以相同字形回复（亦可按对话手动切换）；无法判断时以简体中文回复。其他语言仅在首个市场商业化验证后加入；该门槛在此明示，其他语言的工作不预先获得授权。
